Sir Walter Ralegh (c.1552–1618), The History of the World. This file is Volume I of the 1736 London “Eleventh Edition” (“printed from a Copy revis’d by Himself”), with William Oldys’s life of the author, Ralegh’s 1603 trial, a full contents of all five books, Ralegh’s Preface, chronological-table matter, and the text of Book I only (Beginning and First Ages… from the Creation unto Abraham). ~58,316 lines / ~2.6 MB plain text downloaded from Internet Archive DjVuTXT. ^[source: raleigh_history_of_the_world_vol1_raw.txt]

Ralegh wrote the History in the Tower under James I (first published 1614). For this Tudor knowledge base, the Oldys Life and Trial are the main Elizabethan/Jacobean mining set; Book I is ancient/biblical universal history (Creation → flood → nations → Nimrod/Ninus/Semiramis), useful for Ralegh’s voice and Tower-period thought rather than Tudor narrative proper. ^[source: raleigh_history_of_the_world_vol1_raw.txt]

Contents — mining notes

Oldys Life (~6911–32849)

Long early-modern biography: Devon origins; France/Low Countries service; Humphrey Gilbert and American ventures; Elizabethan court rise; Ireland; Armada-era naval/military reputation; Guiana; James I’s accession; fall, imprisonment, and death. Rich in names, voyages, and faction. Best first stop for elizabeth-i / privateering / court pages. ^[source: raleigh_history_of_the_world_vol1_raw.txt]

Trial (~32850–37170)

Winchester, 17 November 1603: commissioners (Suffolk, Devon, Northampton, Salisbury, etc.); indictment (Cobham, Aremberg, Arabella Stuart, Spanish money); proceedings for high treason. Jacobean aftermath of the Elizabethan career. ^[source: raleigh_history_of_the_world_vol1_raw.txt]

Preface (~338–2523)

Ralegh’s famous preface from the Tower: reputation, fortune, providence, and the use of universal history — including sharp reflections on English kings and contemporary politics. Often mined separately from the biblical books. ^[source: raleigh_history_of_the_world_vol1_raw.txt]
